## Title
Job Portal with Employer Dashboard
## Domain
Web Application / Recruitment & Employment Management
## Who is the user? 
Admin – Manages users, employers, and job postings.
Employer – Posts jobs, manages company profile, and reviews applicants.
Job Seeker – Searches for jobs, uploads a resume, and applies for jobs.
## What problem are we solving?
Many companies still use manual methods or multiple platforms to post jobs and manage applications, making recruitment slow and difficult. Job seekers also spend time searching across different websites for suitable opportunities. This project provides a single platform where employers can post jobs and manage applicants through a dashboard, while job seekers can easily search and apply for jobs. It simplifies the hiring process and improves communication between employers and candidates.
## Proposed Solution
The application will provide secure login for Admin, Employer, and Job Seeker. Employers can create, update, and delete job postings, manage their company profile, and review applications through a dashboard. Job seekers can create profiles, upload resumes, search jobs using filters, and apply online. Admin can monitor users, employers, and job postings to ensure smooth system operation

## User Roles & Permissions
Admin
Manage users

Employer

Job Seeker

## Success Criteria
Users can register and log in successfully.
Employers can post a job in under 2 minutes.
Job seekers can search and apply for a job in under 1 minute.
Employers can view and manage applicants from the dashboard.
Application status updates correctly.
## Out of Scope

AI-based resume analysis
Online salary payment
Real-time chat between employer and candidate
Mobile application
